abstract: >-
  Identifies the source of token-selection signals as a key factor in reliable VLA acceleration. Actuation-slack refresh uses a dense pass during robot execution to renew selection signals and cached states for the next action chunk. This restores closed-loop task success under aggressive token skipping while reducing serving latency by 18–22% relative to dense inference.
